# Event Report: “Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels”

### Event Objectives

- To meet and listen to direct insights from one of the foundational visionaries behind the modern cloud computing era.
- To catch up on the pioneering vision regarding the future of technology, cloud computing, and large-scale distributed systems.
- To deeply inspire the Vietnamese builder community through his famous mantra: **"Now Go Build"**.

### Special Guest & Venue

- **Keynote Speaker:** Dr. **Werner Vogels** – Vice President (VP) and Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Amazon.
- **Venue:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, HCMC.

### Key Highlights

#### Vision on Distributed Systems
Dr. Werner Vogels shared profound perspectives on designing and operating large-scale distributed systems. He emphasized the core elements that help Amazon's systems maintain high availability, limitless scalability, and ultimate flexibility.

#### Customer-Centric Innovation
Innovation does not stem from pursuing technology for technology's sake, but must originate from solving actual customer problems. Every architecture and service created by AWS revolves around this "Customer Obsession" philosophy.

#### The "Now Go Build" Spirit
This is Dr. Werner Vogels' iconic message. He encourages engineers and developers to stop hesitating, reduce pure theorizing, and immediately get hands-on in building solutions that shape the future.

### Key Learnings

- **Design Thinking:** System building must always anticipate failure ("Everything fails, all the time"). Therefore, architectures must be designed to be self-healing and resilient.
- **Dedication & Drive:** Gained a clearer understanding of the role developers/builders play in using technology to solve major societal and business challenges.

### Application to Work (FCAJ Project)

- **Applying System Design Thinking:** Incorporating the "design for failure" philosophy into the FCAJ final project. Focusing more on establishing automated recovery and error-handling mechanisms rather than just writing code for the happy path.
- **Customer-Centric Approach:** When designing features for our AI Agent, the team will prioritize user experience over cramming in unnecessarily complex technologies.

### Personal Experience at the Event

Attending the fireside chat with Dr. Werner Vogels was an unbelievable honor for a young professional just starting their Cloud & AI journey like me.

#### The Approachability of a Tech Legend
Despite being the CTO of one of the world's largest tech corporations, Dr. Werner presented a remarkably approachable and open demeanor. The stories he shared were not dry or purely academic, but highly practical and deeply inspiring.

#### Igniting the "Now Go Build" Motivation
Hearing the "Now Go Build" philosophy directly from its creator infused me with immense motivation. His passion for building and empowering technologists truly touched the hearts of young builders.

#### Key Takeaways
A heartfelt thank you to Dr. Werner Vogels for taking the precious time to visit the AWS HCMC office. His wisdom and pioneering vision proved that the ultimate technology is the one that solves customer pain points in the most simple and resilient way.
