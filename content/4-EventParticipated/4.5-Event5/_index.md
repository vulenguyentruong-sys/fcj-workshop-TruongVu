# Reflection on the "Cloud Mastery" Event

### Purpose of the Event

- To create a networking and practical learning space focused on Cloud Computing for the young tech student community in Saigon.
- To facilitate direct interaction with experts through an open Q&A format, helping students orient their career paths in the Cloud industry.
- To build momentum and spread enthusiasm for the upcoming major event series dedicated to AWS-passionate students.

### Special Guests & Location

- **Speakers:** Experts and Mentors from the AWS community.
- **Location:** AWS Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, HCMC.

### Key Highlights

#### The Heat from the Young Student Community
The event attracted a large number of tech-enthusiastic students from all over Ho Chi Minh City. The dynamic and passionate atmosphere of the youth filled the entire AWS office right from the very first minutes.

#### The "Endless" Discussion Session
The brightest and most memorable highlight of the sharing session was the Q&A segment. Students continuously raised highly practical and sharp questions. The topics ranged from basic orientations for beginners (freshers) to in-depth technical inquiries about Cloud architecture. This enthusiasm made the Q&A session feel vibrant and "endless".

#### An Exciting Knowledge Playground
The event concluded with a mini-game Quiz. Not only did it help everyone review the knowledge they had just acquired, but this game also received an incredibly enthusiastic response, bringing laughter and great bonding to the whole room.

### What I Learned

- **Multi-dimensional Perspectives:** Listening to the questions posed by other students helped me realize many practical aspects that I had never considered during my self-study.
- **Clearer Orientation:** The answers provided by the experts helped me solidify my learning roadmap and specialize my Cloud skills for the future.

### Application to Work (Internship Project)

- **Proactive Questioning Spirit:** From this event, I realized the importance of proactively diving deep into problems. I will actively discuss and ask "tougher" questions to my mentors during the execution of our team's FCAJ Buildrathon project.
- **Broadening Problem-Solving Mindset:** Applying the specialized technical insights gathered from the Q&A session to optimize the current AWS architecture of our project.

### My Experience at the Event

Immersing myself in the Cloud Mastery event was truly an experience that left me feeling **incredibly excited**.

#### A Source of Positive Energy
Meeting peers who share the same burning passion for technology provided me with a massive boost of motivation. The hunger for learning from everyone around me made me realize that I need to push myself even harder.

#### Anticipating New Journeys
The event concluded, yet it sparked a new excitement as I received "leaked information" about the AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026 Workshop happening this coming weekend. This is definitely an appointment I cannot miss!
