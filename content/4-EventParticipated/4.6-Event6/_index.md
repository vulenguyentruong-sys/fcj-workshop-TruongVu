# Reflection on “AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026”

### Purpose of the Event

- To build a dynamic student technology community, fostering active learning and connecting young people passionate about Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- To create a space for students to access practical knowledge from experts and spread an academic spirit among university clubs.
- To mark a special milestone as the largest annual event of the AWS Student Builder Group SGU (ASBG SGU), organized in collaboration with HUFLIT, HCMUTE, and PTIT.

### Special Guests & Location

- **Co-organizers:** ASBG SGU, HUFLIT, HCMUTE, and PTIT.
- **Attendees:** AWS experts, speakers, the Organizing Committee, and a large number of tech students in Ho Chi Minh City.
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City.
- **Date & Time:** Saturday morning, November 21, 2026.
- **Role:** Member (or Organizer/Attendee) from ASBG SGU.

### Key Highlights

#### Gaining Practical Knowledge
The event provided Builders with a valuable opportunity to listen to in-depth sharing about security in enterprise cloud environments, along with inspiring stories about mentoring and developing the next generation of young developers.

#### Spreading the Academic Spirit Across Universities
Representatives from the four AWS Student Builder Groups (HUFLIT, HCMUTE, PTIT, SGU) delivered diverse presentations, sharing their unique technological perspectives and knowledge. This was a platform for student communities to prove their capabilities, proactive spirit, and burning passion for learning.

#### Interaction, Experience, and Bonding
Beyond theory, the auditorium's atmosphere was energized by two minigames: "Quiz Quest" and "Word Quest." These lively interactions brought not only joy and attractive prizes but also broke the ice among students from different universities.

### What I Learned

- **Technical Knowledge:** Grasped the core concepts of Cloud security when applied to real-world enterprise environments.
- **Community Collaboration Skills:** Understood the value of networking, cooperation, and a sense of responsibility when co-organizing a large-scale event with other institutions (HUFLIT, HCMUTE, PTIT).

### Application to Work (Internship Project)

- **Applying Security Standards:** Integrating the enterprise Cloud security knowledge just acquired into auditing and optimizing the system (especially regarding access management and sensitive data) in my current internship project.
- **Teamwork Skills:** Utilizing the collaborative and effective communication skills honed during the event coordination to work seamlessly with internship teammates in solving technical challenges.

### My Experience at the Event

The event was an incredibly proud and moving memory, marking a step of growth for both myself and ASBG SGU.

#### The Pride of an Insider
The three-hour event was the culmination of a long process of effort, coordination, and dedication from the entire Organizing Committee. Witnessing the enthusiasm of the participating students made all the hard work feel completely worthwhile.

#### Borderless Connections
The event created an excellent space to meet students, speakers, and like-minded individuals. The sideline conversations not only expanded my network but also opened up promising opportunities for future collaborations.
