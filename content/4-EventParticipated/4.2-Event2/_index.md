# Event Report: “AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026: HCMC Watch Party”

### Event Objectives

- Catch up with the latest Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence trends directly broadcasted from the main AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 event in Hanoi.
- Create a comprehensive technological experience and networking space for the Cloud & AI Builders community in the Southern region.
- Facilitate live discussion forums and practical problem-solving sessions regarding AI and Cloud computing among interns, developers, and Solution Architects.

### Organizers & Venue

- **Organized by:** AWS Study Group.
- **Venue:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th & 36th Floors, Bitexco Financial Tower, HCMC.
- **Scale:** Over 300 builders, developers, and tech experts.

### Key Highlights

#### Seamless Knowledge Connection via Hanoi Keynote
- Live-streamed valuable insights and keynote sessions from top AWS leaders and industry experts.
- Gained deep insights into three technological focal points:
  + The emerging wave of **Agentic AI** (Autonomous AI).
  + Infrastructure optimization strategies for **Data Foundations**.
  + Enterprise-scale Cloud Modernization strategies.

#### Live "In-the-Trenches" Discussions
- Instead of just passively watching a livestream, builders and Solution Architects (SA) in HCMC created highly interactive "roundtable" discussions during breaks.
- Deep-dived into the challenges of moving **AI Agents** from experimental (sandbox/lab) environments to real-world production.
- Actively debated and shared practical experiences regarding the evergreen topic: **Cloud Cost Optimization**.

#### Networking & Spreading Community Spirit
- Built a strong professional network through constant handshakes and greetings within the cozy yet professional AWS Office space.
- Reaffirmed the core value of the AWS Study Group: Building a community where knowledge is freely shared, serving as a launchpad for every builder's career.

### Key Learnings

#### Technological Mindset & Solutions
- **Agentic AI:** Understood the paradigm shift from standard Generative AI (content generation) to Agentic AI (AI capable of reasoning and autonomously executing task chains).
- **Data Foundation:** Recognized that for AI to operate accurately and effectively, enterprises must possess a clean, well-organized, and highly secure Data Foundation on the Cloud.

#### Practical Skills
- Absorbed valuable tips and best practices for Cost Optimization from real-world case studies shared by senior Devs and SAs during the roundtable discussions.

### Application to Work (FCAJ / Capstone Project)

- **Data Pipeline Design:** Will prioritize designing a proper and scalable Data Foundation right from the beginning of the final capstone project.
- **AI Agent Deployment:** Apply practical perspectives gained from the discussions to develop the team's AI Bot during the Buildrathon, ensuring it is not just functional but highly applicable.
- **Cost Management (FinOps):** Strictly apply the shared AWS cost optimization experiences to effectively manage the $100 AWS Credit budget.

### Personal Experience at the Event

The Watch Party on September 29th brought a completely unique vibe. Even though the main event was taking place in Hanoi, the heat on the 26th and 36th floors of the Bitexco tower was equally intense.

#### Multi-dimensional Discussion Experience
What impressed me the most was not the large livestream screen, but the interactive spirit of the AWS Study Group community. The impromptu "roundtable" discussions between interns, Developers, and Solution Architects helped answer many of my questions about running AI Agents in production—things rarely covered in textbooks.

#### Community Bonding Atmosphere
The AWS office space provided a professional yet warm environment. Seeing over 300 fellow builders put aside their busy weekends to gather and absorb new knowledge together infused me with incredibly positive energy.

#### Key Takeaways
I realized that while self-study is crucial in the tech industry, participating in communities and listening to the "hard-earned" experiences of seniors significantly reduces the time spent on trial and error.
