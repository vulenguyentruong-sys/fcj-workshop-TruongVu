### General Evaluation

**1. Work Environment**  
The environment at FCAJ is truly dynamic and embodies the "builder" spirit. Having access to co-working spaces and attending in-person events at the AWS office (Bitexco) provided a highly professional feel. The other interns are also extremely passionate, creating a very vibrant academic atmosphere.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admins**  
I am very grateful to the mentors. Their guidance isn't about micromanaging or just handing over pre-written code; it's about provoking architectural thinking (like when reviewing our team's MediMind AI project) and teaching us how to navigate AWS Documentation. The team admins are also very attentive in keeping us updated on events and lab progress.

**3. Alignment between Work and Major**  
It aligns perfectly and even goes far beyond my university curriculum. School provides the foundation in AI, but FCAJ taught me how to bring those AI models to a real-world Cloud environment (Serverless, Docker, ECS) so they can serve thousands of users, rather than just running locally on my laptop.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
The amount of knowledge I've absorbed over the past 3 months probably equals a year of self-study. I learned not only technical skills (AWS CloudFormation, CI/CD, FinOps) but also remote teamwork, how to pitch technical ideas, and the "Customer Obsession" mindset from AWS events.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
Team spirit was most evident during the days our group was rushing to meet the FCAJ Buildrathon deadline. Despite facing tough infrastructure deployment errors, no one got discouraged; we were willing to stay online late into the night to debug together. The knowledge-sharing culture within the AWS Study Group community is also a fantastic highlight.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
Being provided with an AWS account (along with Credits) to freely practice Labs without worrying about costs is an incredible benefit for students. Furthermore, the t-shirts and swags from Meetups and AWS Community Days served as a huge morale booster.


### Other Questions

**What were you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
The shift in my mindset. I transitioned from merely coding features to actively considering how a system is deployed, how it is secured (IAM, WAF), and how much it will cost to maintain monthly (FinOps).

**What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**  
I think the program could add a few live "Troubleshooting" workshops. Sometimes students get stuck on very minor errors (like misconfiguring a Security Group or IAM Role) and have to struggle for quite a while. A dedicated session for common pitfalls would save a lot of time.

**Would you recommend this internship to your friends? Why?**  
100% yes. This is not just an internship to get a stamp for a school report; it's a real stepping stone into the Cloud and DevOps industry. The environment, knowledge, and network gained from FCAJ are invaluable for students.


### Proposals & Wishes

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
I hope in future seasons, the organizing committee can create smaller, specialized "Study Groups" (around 5-7 people) based on specific topics right from the beginning of the course (e.g., AI/ML group, Serverless group, Container group). This would make it easier for students with similar goals to interact and cross-discuss.

**Would you like to continue with this program in the future?**  
I strongly desire to continue my journey with the AWS community, perhaps in a role supporting future cohorts or participating in more advanced projects developing GenAI solutions on AWS.

**Other feedback:**  
Thank you to FCAJ and the AWS Student Builder Group community for creating such a high-quality technology playground for students. "Now Go Build"!
